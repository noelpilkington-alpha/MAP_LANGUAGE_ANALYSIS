Definitions

  • Growth. Growth X = (best valid RIT in term − NWEA baseline) ÷ NWEA projected growth; 2x is the target. First-test X and NWEA conditional growth percentile shown as reference.
  • Validity. Tests marked invalidated, to-be-deleted, or failing NWEA's validity gate are excluded.
  • Level. Level at time of test from the grade NWEA recorded on that test (K-1 LL, 2-3 L1, 4-5 L2, 6-8 MS, 9-12 HS); current level from Timeback.
  • Campus. Current campus and segment from the student's Timeback primary org; test/sandbox orgs excluded.
  • Xp. Writing XP per school day = (XP on AlphaWrite line-item results + XP on Writing-subject results, by score date) ÷ school days in the window.
  • Exposure. Enrollment days from dated enrollments in Language (by platform), Writing and Vocabulary courses within the window.

Writing XP goal: 15 XP per school day. School days per window: ftw 74, wtw 74, fts 182, wts 108, sts 182, ftf 182, s3 34, sy2627 32. Writing test pass threshold: 90.

Data sources (all pulled from the Timeback OneRoster API on September 24, 2026)

  • MAP results: map_results_2026-09-24.csv — 11830 Language rows, 11624 after excluding invalidated, to-be-deleted and NWEA-invalid tests.
  • Student directory with current level, primary org, status and admission date (rostering/students).
  • Writing XP: 139411 gradebook results for 3141 students since August 2025 (AlphaWrite line items plus Writing-subject results). Validated against the Session 3 source-of-truth export.
  • Enrollments with begin and end dates for 48250 Language, Writing and Vocabulary enrollments; courses and orgs for classification.
  • Writing test results: writing-results-2026-09-24.csv.

Students in the analysis: 6621 (Virtual program 2685, Partner district 1859, Alpha campus (in-person) 1181, Affiliate school 569, Sports academy (in-person) 327).

What changed from the January 30, 2026 report

  • The whole site is generated from one pipeline; the original pages are archived unchanged under Winter 2026 Report (archive). Winter numbers here use the final data set (after retakes), so they differ from the January snapshot.
  • Scope widened from 19 campuses to the whole in-person network, with virtual programs and partner districts reported separately.
  • Level is taken at time of test; campus from Timeback; tests flagged invalid are excluded.
  • Writing XP is measured for every student from the gradebook for the whole window, not from a six-week export for Writing-enrolled students only.
  • Platform and program flags use dated enrollments (days in window) rather than a February snapshot.
  • "Below expected" uses NWEA's projection because the internal prediction export failed; retake rule two is flagged as "check prediction" for the same reason.
  • Group comparisons carry rank-based tests, and the XP relationship is estimated with a logistic model on the 2x target controlling for starting RIT, level and segment, with campus-clustered errors.

Audit

An independent recomputation and a check battery (join integrity, validity flags, attrition, outlier sensitivity, temporal ordering, multiple comparisons) were run on September 24, 2026; see ANALYSIS_AUDIT_2026-09-24.md in the project folder. Known limits: observational data, a ratio metric with small denominators at HS, Fall 2026 testing still open.
