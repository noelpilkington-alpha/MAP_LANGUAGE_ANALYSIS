What this page is

The rest of this dashboard is the Winter 2026 report (January 30, 2026). This page re-runs its core analyses on the latest Language MAP data pulled from Timeback on September 24, 2026: the Spring 2026 end-of-year tests that close out SY 2025-26, and the Fall 2026 beginning-of-year tests that open SY 2026-27.

  • Who is included. Students whose 2025-26 roster campus or current MAP school is one of the 19 campuses in the Winter report (898 students with a Language MAP result; 401 tested in Spring 2026, 780 in Fall 2026). Students at the 32 Alpha campuses that opened since the Winter report (403 students with a Fall 2026 Language test) are not included so the numbers stay comparable.
  • Growth X = observed RIT growth ÷ NWEA projected growth for the window, as in the Winter report. The highest RIT in a term counts (the retake convention); first-test values are shown alongside. NWEA only supplies a growth window when it has the earlier test on file, so growth n is smaller than tested n.
  • Levels. Spring 2026 uses the 2025-26 roster level. Fall 2026 uses the student's current grade (2-3 L1, 4-5 L2, 6-8 MS, 9-12 HS), so students have moved up a level between the two halves of this page.
  • HS caution. NWEA projected growth at high school is often 0-2 RIT points, so HS multiples swing wildly; read HS medians and "met projection" rather than means.
  • Fall 2026 is still in progress. Latest test in the pull: 2026-09-22. Retakes can still raise Fall scores.
