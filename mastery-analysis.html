Scope. Headline tables cover the in-person network: Alpha campuses, affiliate schools (GT, Strata, Nova, NextGen, Montessorium and similar) and in-person sports academies, 2077 students with a valid Language MAP since Fall 2025. Virtual programs (2685 students) and partner districts (1859) are reported separately in aggregate. Growth X = (best valid RIT in the term − NWEA baseline) ÷ NWEA projected growth; 2x is the target. Tests flagged invalid by NWEA or Timeback are excluded. See Methods & Data.
Mastery = passed at least one Alpha Standardized Writing test (score 90+) between August 2025 and June 2026, from writing-results-2026-09-24.csv. Non-HS students only.
